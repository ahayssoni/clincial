A calm Pomodoro timer for studying. It floats above your work, covers your screens in a soft green veil when it's time to rest, and can turn a second monitor into a full-screen focus display.

## Download

| | |
|---|---|
| **Mac** (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `Moss-mac.dmg` |
| **Windows** 10 and 11 (64-bit) | `Moss-Windows-Setup.exe` |

## Install on Mac

1. Open `Moss-mac.dmg` and drag **Moss** into **Applications**.
2. Open Moss from Applications. macOS says it can't verify the developer, because Moss isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway** next to Moss, and confirm.

You only do this once. If you prefer Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Moss.app`

Moss lives in the menu bar (top right) and the floating timer appears on your screen.

## Install on Windows

1. Run `Moss-Windows-Setup.exe`. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**. Moss isn't signed with a paid certificate.
2. Moss installs and opens by itself. It lives in the system tray: click **^** next to the clock, and drag the Moss icon onto the taskbar to keep it in view.

## First minute

- Hover the floating timer and press play. Drag it anywhere; it remembers where you put it.
- The screen button opens the full-screen focus display, by default on your other monitor.
- Right-click the timer for the menu and Settings.
- Start or pause from anywhere: `⌘⌥⇧P` on Mac, `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+P` on Windows.
